A recovery attempt must rebuild Final EWA under the replacement device epoch. It may not reuse the old texture, view, source capability, field handle, device-local identity, or Analysis receipt.

## 13. Disposal authority

Success disposal order:

```text
1 verify Analysis publication ownership transfer
2 release invocation-private QSR03F references
3 destroy compact target
4 retire Final EWA source capability
5 dispose QSR03B arena
6 release per-attempt bindings and observers
7 release device lease
8 close attempt state
```

The Analysis-owned published QMap is not destroyed by the invocation.

Assembly failure disposes in reverse construction order. An unsubmitted encoder is discarded immediately. Submitted work is fence-drained before resource retirement. Device loss invalidates logical ownership without pretending the lost device completed work.

Disposal is terminal and duplicate disposal is rejected. No private resource may remain after failure or cancellation.
